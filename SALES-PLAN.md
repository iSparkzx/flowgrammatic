## 1. Executive Summary

### The Opportunity
The AI automation market is exploding. According to BCG, 67% of C-level executives consider autonomous agents crucial to their AI transformation, and the hyperautomation market is projected to grow from $12.8B (2024) to $41.2B by 2033. Companies that automate early gain massive competitive advantages.

### FlowGrammatic's Position
FlowGrammatic offers a compelling value proposition:
- **73% cost reduction** in automated processes
- **10x faster operations**
- **95% error reduction**
- **300% average ROI**
- **4-8 week deployment timeline**

### First Customer Goal
**Target:** Sign first paying customer within 30-45 days  
**Ideal Starting Point:** Starter tier ($2,500/mo) or Growth tier ($5,000-8,000/mo)  
**Strategy:** High-touch, personalized outreach to warm network + targeted cold outreach to high-probability verticals

### Key Success Factors
1. **Lead with pain, not technology** - Prospects don't buy AI; they buy solutions to problems
2. **Show ROI fast** - Calculate specific dollar savings in every conversation
3. **Start with quick wins** - Target "low-hanging fruit" automations that deliver visible results in weeks
4. **Build case studies immediately** - Document everything for social proof

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### 3.1 Company-Level Criteria

**MUST HAVE (Non-Negotiable)**
- ✅ 25-250 employees
- ✅ $3M+ annual revenue
- ✅ Uses cloud-based tools (SaaS stack)
- ✅ Growing (hiring, expanding, raised funding)
- ✅ Has identifiable repetitive processes
- ✅ Located in US, Canada, UK, or Australia

**SHOULD HAVE (Strong Indicators)**
- ✅ Recently hired operations, CS, or IT leadership
- ✅ Uses integrateable tools (HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, etc.)
- ✅ Active on LinkedIn (leadership posts about growth/scaling)
- ✅ Recently raised funding (for startups)
- ✅ Mentions "scaling," "automation," or "efficiency" on website/job posts

**RED FLAGS (Avoid)**
- ❌ Less than 15 employees (budget constraints)
- ❌ Declining revenue or layoffs
- ❌ No cloud tools (legacy systems only)
- ❌ Government/highly regulated without compliance expertise
- ❌ No clear decision-maker reachable

## 5. Lead Qualification Framework

### 5.1 BANT+ Framework (Modified for FlowGrammatic)

Use this framework during initial conversations to qualify leads:

| Criteria | Question to Ask | What You're Looking For | Score |
|----------|----------------|------------------------|-------|
| **B - Budget** | "What range are you considering for an automation investment?" | $2K+/month available or ROI-justified | 0-25 |
| **A - Authority** | "Who else would be involved in this decision?" | Can they approve or do we need executive buy-in? | 0-25 |
| **N - Need** | "What process is causing the most pain right now?" | Specific, urgent problem we can solve | 0-25 |
| **T - Timeline** | "When would you ideally want this live?" | Within 90 days = hot; 6mo+ = nurture | 0-15 |
| **I - Impact** | "What would solving this mean for your team?" | Quantifiable savings (time, money, headcount) | 0-10 |

**Score Interpretation:**
- **80-100:** Hot lead - Fast track to proposal
- **60-79:** Warm lead - Needs nurturing/more discovery
- **40-59:** Cool lead - Add to drip sequence
- **<40:** Not qualified - Politely decline

### 5.2 Qualification Questions by Stage

**Stage 1: Initial Contact (LinkedIn/Email)**
- What's your biggest operational headache right now?
- How much time does your team spend on manual tasks?
- Have you explored automation before?

**Stage 2: Discovery Call**
- Walk me through [specific process] end-to-end
- How many people touch this process?
- What happens when it breaks or delays?
- What have you tried before?
- What does success look like?
- Who needs to approve this investment?
- What's your timeline for solving this?

**Stage 3: Pre-Proposal**
- Can you quantify the cost of the current problem?
- What's your budget range for a solution?
- What concerns might [other stakeholder] have?
- What would prevent you from moving forward?

### 5.3 Lead Scoring Matrix

Create a simple spreadsheet to score leads:

| Factor | Weight | Score Range |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| ICP Company Match | 20% | 1-5 |
| Decision-Maker Access | 20% | 1-5 |
| Problem Urgency | 25% | 1-5 |
| Budget Confirmed | 20% | 1-5 |
| Timeline (<90 days) | 15% | 1-5 |

**Calculation:** `Total Score = (ICP×20% + DM×20% + Urgency×25% + Budget×20% + Timeline×15%) × 20`

Example: `(4×.20 + 5×.20 + 4×.25 + 3×.20 + 5×.15) × 20 = 81 points`

## 7. Sales Process

### 7.1 Discovery Call Script (30 Minutes)

**Pre-Call Prep (5 minutes before):**
- Review LinkedIn, company website, recent news
- Note potential pain points based on ICP
- Prepare 3 specific questions about their business
- Have ROI calculator ready

**Opening (3 minutes)**
```
"[Name], thanks for taking the time. Before we dive in, I want 
to make sure this is valuable for you — not just a pitch fest.

My goal for this call is to understand what you're dealing with 
and see if there's a fit. If there is, great. If not, I'll tell 
you — and maybe point you in a better direction.

Sound fair?"
```

**Rapport + Context (5 minutes)**
```
"First, I'd love to learn more about [Company]. I saw [specific 
observation from research]. Can you tell me more about [their role / 
current focus]?"

"What made you take this call today? What's top of mind?"
```

**Pain Discovery (10 minutes)**

Use the SPIN technique:
- **Situation:** "Walk me through how [process] works today."
- **Problem:** "Where does it break down? What frustrates your team?"
- **Implication:** "What happens when [problem]? What's the cost?"
- **Need-Payoff:** "If you could fix this, what would that mean for you?"

Key questions:
1. "What's the most tedious part of your team's week?"
2. "How many hours does [process] take per week? Per month?"
3. "What would your team do with that time if they got it back?"
4. "What have you tried before to solve this?"
5. "What's the cost of not fixing this?"

**Quantify the Pain (5 minutes)**
```
"Let me make sure I understand. You said [process] takes [X hours/week] 
and involves [Y people]. At an average loaded cost of $[Z]/hour, 
that's roughly $[calculation] per month just on this one workflow.

And you mentioned errors cost you [$ amount] in [refunds/rework/etc].

So conservatively, we're looking at [$total] per month in pain.

Does that sound right?"
```

**Solution Overview (5 minutes)**
```
"Based on what you've shared, here's what I'm thinking...

[Describe 1-2 specific automation solutions tailored to their pain]

Typically, this takes [X weeks] to implement and clients see 
[specific results - time saved, costs cut, errors reduced].

For a company like yours, we'd probably be looking at our 
[Starter/Growth] tier — which runs [$X-Y/month].

Given the [$pain amount] you're spending now, that's an ROI 
of [X]x in the first year alone."
```

**Next Steps (2 minutes)**
```
"If this sounds interesting, here's what I suggest:

1. I'll put together a quick proposal outlining the specific 
   workflows we'd tackle, timeline, and expected results.

2. We can schedule a follow-up call to walk through it and 
   answer any questions.

3. If it makes sense, we move forward. If not, no hard feelings.

Does that work? Who else should be on that next call?"
```

## 8. Objection Handling Guide

### 8.1 Price Objections

**"It's too expensive"**
```
"I hear you. Let's look at it differently.

You mentioned [process] costs you [$X/month] in labor and errors. 
Our solution is [$Y/month]. That means you're getting [$Z/month] 
back — plus your team's sanity.

Is there a budget number that would work? Let's see if we can 
structure something that makes the ROI undeniable."
```

**"We don't have budget for this"**
```
"Understood. But let me ask — do you have budget for the 
problem?

You're spending [$X/month] on [manual process/errors/overtime]. 
If we could prove ROI in the first 60 days, would that change 
the conversation?

Some clients start with a smaller pilot — one workflow — to 
prove value before committing to a larger engagement."
```

**"Can you do it cheaper?"**
```
"What did you have in mind?

Here's how I think about it: You can pay less for something 
that doesn't work, or invest in something that pays for itself.

That said, if budget is tight, we could start with fewer 
workflows and expand later. Would that work?"
```

### 8.2 Timing Objections

**"Now's not a good time"**
```
"I totally get it. When would be a better time?

One thing to consider though — every week this stays manual 
is another [$X] in costs. In 6 months, that's [$6X].

Would it make sense to at least do the audit now, so you're
```

## 9. Pricing & Negotiation Strategy

### 9.1 Value-Based Pricing Philosophy

**The Golden Rule:** Price based on value delivered, not hours worked.

**Formula:**
```
Price = (Customer's Annual Cost of Problem × 0.2 to 0.3)
```

If automation saves them $100K/year, charging $20-30K/year is a 3-5x ROI — a no-brainer.

### 9.2 Pricing Tiers (From Website)

| Tier | Monthly Price | Best For | Included |
|------|---------------|----------|----------|
| **Starter** | $2,500/mo | First automation, small teams | 3 workflows, 1 chatbot, basic analytics, email support |
| **Growth** | $5,000-$8,000/mo | Scaling companies | 10 workflows, 3 AI agents, advanced analytics, Slack support, strategy calls |
| **Enterprise** | $10,000-$20,000/mo | Full transformation | Unlimited, custom ML, dedicated engineer, SLA, executive partnership |

### 9.3 Pricing Strategies

**Anchor High, Land in the Middle**
```
"For a full transformation, we typically work in the $10-20K range. 
Given your immediate needs, I'd recommend starting with our Growth 
tier at $6K/month — it covers everything you need and leaves room 
to expand."
```

**ROI Framing**
```
"You're spending $15K/month on this process. At $5K/month for 
automation, you're cash-flow positive from month one with a 
3x return by year end."
```

**Payment Structure Options**
- Monthly retainer (standard)
- Quarterly with 5% discount
- Annual with 10-15% discount
- Pilot project (fixed price) → retainer

### 9.4 Negotiation Tactics

**If They Want Discount:**
```
"I can work on price if we adjust scope. What if we started 
with [reduced scope] and expanded once you see results?"
```

**If They Want Pilot:**
```
"I love pilots. Let's do 6 weeks on one workflow at [$X]. 
If we hit [specific metric], we roll into a full engagement. 
If not, you walk away with a working automation."
```

**If They Want Performance-Based:**
```
"I'm open to performance-based pricing. How about [base price] 
plus a success bonus when we hit [specific measurable target]? 
That way we both have skin in the game."
```

### 9.5 Contract Terms

**Standard Terms:**
- Month-to-month after initial 3-month commitment
- 30-day notice to cancel
- Payment due first of month
- Net 15 terms for invoices

**For First Customer (Flexibility Allowed):**
- Month-to-month from start (lower risk for them)
- Performance guarantee (refund if X not achieved in 60 days)
- Testimonial/case study rights in exchange for discount
